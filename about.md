I'm **Dean Chen**, a Software Engineer at **Google** in the San Francisco Bay Area, working on open-source AI Agent infrastructure (**[`google/adk-python`](https://github.com/google/adk-python)** — see my Google Cloud Blog deep dive on **[Graph workflows in ADK](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/developers-practitioners/graph-workflows-in-adk-everything-you-need-to-know/)**). Outside of work, I enjoy **vibecoding** — spending hours turning random curiosities into interactive web apps, games, and digital experiments without feeling tired.

### Why I Build What I Build (The Backstory)

Almost every side project on [`deanchen.me`](/) traces back to an offline obsession:

* **History & Geography Nerd ("Closet Liberal Arts Major")**: I love history (especially China's Northern and Southern Dynasties / 南北朝) and human geography — I probably would have majored in the humanities if high-school politics hadn't been so hard to memorize. That obsession led to **[江山时序 · Jiangshan Historical Atlas](/jiangshan-map/)**.
* **Non-Gamer Building a Game**: I rarely play mainstream video games, yet I couldn't stop building my own event-driven life simulator — **[硅谷人生重开模拟器 · SV Life Reboot](/sv-life-reboot/)**, modeling the real Silicon Valley grind from school selection and H1B lotteries to green cards and FIRE.
* **Travel & Miles Optimizer**: I face a classic Bay Area paradox — I'm too lazy to drive an hour up to San Francisco for an event, yet I'll happily research multi-stop layovers and snipe business-class award flights to Asia. That hobby inspired **[Credit Card Benefits Tracker](/cc-benefits-tracker/)**.
* **Football & Messi Fan**: Being a longtime Leo Messi fan naturally sparked **[World Cup Interactive Map](/world-cup-map/)**.
